% Introduction part II
This part of the thesis is based on the work done in our specialization project. The project is the foundation for inventing and developing our chosen concept Hover. First, an introduction to general game development concepts is given. Specifically, how to design an enjoyable game, player types, and game reward systems. After that, a definition, common characteristics, and a description of existing alternative games are presented. Further on, a technology review exploring the possibilities in alternative games’ field and relevant technologies is conducted. At last, all the potential concepts we invented during the specialization project conclude the part.
