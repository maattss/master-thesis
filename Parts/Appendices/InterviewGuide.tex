\section{Interview Guide}
\label{appendix:interview_guide}

\textit{For semi-structured interviews. Used for individual and group interviews.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item What thoughts do you have about the game?
    \item What motivated you to use the Hover?
    \item How were you affected by points, ranking leaderboard, and reactions? 
    \item How were you affected by the presence of friends?
    \item How was your use of the game affected by the national restrictions?
    \item Anything else you want to address?
\end{enumerate}
