\section{Functional Requirements}
\label{chap:requirements}
To organize and prioritize features in Hover, we have created three sets of functional requirements (FR) based on their importance; \textit{high}, \textit{medium}, and \textit{low}. This chapter will present and briefly explain the idea behind the categories.

\subsection{High Priority FRs}
High-priority Functional Requirements are needed for the game to function as intended. Consequently, the requirements in Table \ref{tab:FR_high} are the ones that we categorize as a must-have in the application before testing it on real users. Thus, all high-priority FRs are implemented in Hover.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\small}c|X|}
    \hline
        \textbf{\#} & \multicolumn{1}{c|}{\textbf{Functional Requirement}} \\ \hline
        \textbf{FR1} & Users should be able to create an account and log in. \\
        \textbf{FR2} & Users should be able to view hover zones (geofences) on a map. \\
        \textbf{FR3} & Users should be able to track activities. \\
        \textbf{FR4} & Users should be able to publish activities to the feed. \\
        \textbf{FR5} & Users should be able to create new challenges. \\
        \textbf{FR6} & Users should be able to accept or decline challenge invites. \\
        \textbf{FR7} & Users should be able to see their progress in ongoing challenges. \\
        \textbf{FR8} & Challenges should be validated and checked for expiry when needed. \\
        \textbf{FR9} & Challenges should be published to the feed when finished. \\
        \textbf{FR10} & Users should be able to earn achievements based on their activities. \\
        \textbf{FR11} & Achievements should be published to the feed. \\
        \textbf{FR12} & Users should be able to view activities, achievements, and completed challenges in the feed. \\
        \textbf{FR13} & Users should be able to compare their score with friends on a leaderboard. \\
        \textbf{FR14} & Users should be able to view their profile (displaying achievements, scores, activities, etc.). \\ \hline
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{High priority functional requirements.}
    \label{tab:FR_high}
    \vspace*{-\baselineskip}
\end{table}

\subsection{Medium Priority FRs}
Medium priority FRs are requirements that should be implemented if we have time. They improve the game experience but are not crucial for the game to function as intended. Most of the medium priority FRs in Table \ref{tab:FR_medium} have been implemented. The only two exceptions are FR23 and FR24.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\small}c|X|}
        \hline
        \textbf{\#} & \multicolumn{1}{c|}{\textbf{Functional Requirement}} \\ \hline
        \textbf{FR15} & Users should be able to track together with a friend to boost their points. \\ 
        \textbf{FR16} & Users should be able to pause, resume and discard ongoing activities.\\
        \textbf{FR17} & Users should be notified of important events (when entering or leaving a hover zone, etc.). \\
        \textbf{FR18} & Users should be able to interact with feed elements (react to, open profiles, etc.). \\
        \textbf{FR19} & Users should be able to customize their profile (name, bio, avatar). \\
        \textbf{FR20} & Users should be able to visit other players’ profiles. \\
        \textbf{FR21} & Users should be able to earn streaks for tracking daily. \\
        \textbf{FR22} & Users should be able to view information about hover zones. \\
        \textbf{FR23} & Users should be able to compete in open challenges.\\
        \textbf{FR24} & Users should be able to see their completed challenges. \\ \hline
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Medium priority functional requirements.}
    \label{tab:FR_medium}
    \vspace*{-\baselineskip}
\end{table}

\subsection{Low Priority FRs}
Low priority functional requirements are nice to have features. They could improve the game, but not in a dramatic way. In this project, the requirements categorized with low priority have not been implemented (see Table \ref{tab:FR_low}). However, they serve as good suggestions for further work, as we will describe later in Chapter \ref{chap:further_work}. 

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\small}c|X|}
        \hline
        \textbf{\#} & \multicolumn{1}{c|}{\textbf{Functional Requirement}} \\ \hline
        \textbf{FR25} & Users should be able to customize their list of friends. \\ 
        \textbf{FR26} & Users should be able to sign up for in-game clubs. \\ 
        \textbf{FR27} & Users should be able to earn real-world awards.\\
        \textbf{FR28} & Users should be able to evolve a character based on their score. \\
        \textbf{FR29} & Users should be able to log in using external services (Facebook, Google, etc.) \\ 
        \textbf{FR30} & Users should be able to comment on feed elements.\\ \hline
    \end{tabularx}
    \caption{Low priority functional requirements.}
    \label{tab:FR_low}
    \vspace*{-\baselineskip}
\end{table}
